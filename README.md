## 👋 Welcome

**AI Tinkerers Luxembourg** brings together developers, engineers, researchers, founders, and technical makers who are *actually building* with artificial intelligence.

This is a space for practical work: running experiments, sharing working prototypes, learning from failed attempts, and meeting people who understand the technical challenges behind AI products.

**Less hype. More hands-on building.**

> **Chapter status:** The official Luxembourg page currently says **“AI Tinkerers is coming to Luxembourg!”** Subscribe there for announcements and future event details. Dates, venues, and schedules are not confirmed in this README.

## 🎤 What to expect

AI Tinkerers events are designed around **builders and their work**, rather than marketing presentations.

- **Live technical demos:** See working code, prototypes, and systems in action.
- **Honest engineering discussions:** Talk about architecture, trade-offs, failures, and lessons learned.
- **Practical collaboration:** Find other people exploring similar problems.
- **Builder-first networking:** Meet local AI practitioners without sales pitches.

A prototype does not have to be polished to be interesting. An honest breakdown of what failed can be just as useful as a successful launch.

## 🙌 Get involved

**Attend a meetup**  
Visit the [Luxembourg chapter page](https://luxembourg.aitinkerers.org/) and subscribe for announcements.

**Share a demo**  
Have a working AI experiment, prototype, or product? Bring the technical story: the problem, what you built, how it works, and what you learned. Watch the chapter page for event-specific demo submissions.

**Contribute on GitHub**  
Use this repository to propose community resources, suggest technical topics, share useful open-source projects, and improve documentation. Open an issue to discuss an idea or a pull request for a concrete improvement.

**Help build the community**  
Interested in supporting a session, volunteering, or collaborating? Contact the local organizers via our [contact page](CONTACT.md).

## 💡 Suggested demo format

If you're preparing a demo, a simple structure works well:

1. **The problem** — What were you trying to solve?
2. **The build** — What did you create, and which models or tools did you use?
3. **The demo** — Show the system running, ideally with real inputs.
4. **The lessons** — What worked? What broke? What would you change?
5. **The next step** — What would you like to explore or improve?

Technical substance matters more than polished slides. **No sales pitches.**

## 🤝 Contributing to this repository

We welcome contributions that make the local AI builder community more useful.

- **Share:** Link to a relevant open-source project, demo, tutorial, or reproducible experiment.
- **Improve:** Fix unclear documentation, broken links, or outdated material.
- **Suggest:** Open an issue for an idea, session topic, or community resource.
- **Collaborate:** Discuss your proposal respectfully before making large changes.

Please review our **[Contribution Guide](CONTRIBUTING.md)** before submitting a pull request. Briefly explain what changed and why it helps other builders. Only submit content and code you have permission to share; never include API keys, credentials, private datasets, or someone else's confidential material.
