# Code of Conduct

**AI Tinkerers Luxembourg** is a community for people who build, experiment, and learn with artificial intelligence. We want our GitHub spaces, meetups, demos, and discussions to be welcoming, technically rigorous, and respectful.

This document complements the [global AI Tinkerers Code of Conduct](https://aitinkerers.org/p/ai-tinkerers-code-of-conduct). Participants should follow both; community organizers may apply additional event-specific rules.

## Our commitment

We welcome participants regardless of experience level, background, identity, or affiliation. Everyone should be able to ask questions, share unfinished work, give constructive feedback, and participate without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- **Be respectful.** Challenge ideas, results, and technical claims—not the people behind them.
- **Make room for others.** Invite questions, avoid gatekeeping, and help newcomers participate.
- **Give useful feedback.** Be specific, constructive, and open to being corrected.
- **Credit collaborators.** Respect attribution, licenses, intellectual property, and others' work.
- **Respect privacy and consent.** Ask before photographing, recording, or publishing identifiable information from community events. Do not share private data, credentials, or confidential material in demos or repositories.
- **Be transparent about AI results.** Clearly distinguish working demonstrations from simulations, benchmarks from anecdotes, and personal opinions from verified findings.
- **Keep it builder-focused.** Share real technical work and lessons learned; avoid spam, unsolicited promotion, and aggressive sales or recruitment pitches.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, stalking, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized comments or imagery, unwelcome advances, or unwanted physical contact.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, events, or collaborative work.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission (doxxing).
- Repeated spam, deceptive promotion, plagiarism, or knowingly misleading technical claims.
- Using community channels to distribute malicious code, stolen data, or instructions intended to harm others.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to community-maintained repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, chat spaces, virtual sessions, and in-person events. It also applies when someone represents AI Tinkerers Luxembourg in a public community capacity.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness conduct that violates these guidelines:

1. **If there is immediate danger**, contact local emergency services or venue staff.
2. **For non-emergency concerns**, contact the Luxembourg chapter organizers privately using the contact options on the [official chapter website](https://luxembourg.aitinkerers.org/). If a repository lists a private maintainer contact, you may use that too.
3. Share only the details you are comfortable providing, such as what happened, when it happened, and relevant messages or links.

**Do not disclose sensitive allegations or personal information in public GitHub issues.** If no private channel is listed, you can request a private contact method without sharing incident details publicly.

Organizers will aim to review reports fairly, limit access to sensitive details, and take proportionate action. Confidentiality will be respected as far as reasonably possible, but cannot be guaranteed in every situation. People involved in a report should not investigate or decide their own case.

## Enforcement

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include a private warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restrictions, removal from a project or event, or a community ban. Serious or repeated violations may lead to immediate restrictions.

We may update these guidelines as the community grows.
